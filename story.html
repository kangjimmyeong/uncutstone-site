Our Story

Seven Puzzle was a classroom tool before it was a game

It started in real classrooms

For years, gifted-education classrooms in Korea have used a hands-on wooden puzzle called Seven Puzzle to train spatial reasoning. Children turn seven simple pieces in their hands, read a blueprint, and discover how they become one perfect cube.

A decade in education

Founder Jinmyeong Kang has built education companies since college. His cooperative worked with over 400 schools across Korea — and its motto became this game's design philosophy.

  • 2012Founds In-Us as a university startup club in Incheon
  • 2016Selected as an outstanding team in the Ministry of Economy and Finance's 1st Youth Cooperative Startup Program
  • 2018In-Us works with 400+ schools nationwide; commended by the Mayor of Incheon; designated a village enterprise by the Ministry of the Interior and Safety (press: Bridge Economy, Incheon Ilbo)
  • 2019Featured as the subject of an MBN television documentary — watch on Apple TV
  • 2026Korea Education Contents Lab founds Uncut Stone Studio to rebuild the classroom tool as a game

The classroom tool, in your hand

Seven Puzzle the game keeps everything the classroom taught us: no timers, no lives, no pressure — just seven pieces, a blueprint, and the quiet click of a piece finding its place. 200 stages, 12 languages, made for the calm minutes before sleep.

“Education is slow, but certain.”
