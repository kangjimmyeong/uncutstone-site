Our Story

A classroom puzzle, carried softly into the palm of your hand

The puzzle began in a classroom

Seven Puzzle began as a teacher's tool: seven numbered wooden blocks, made for children to hold, turn, and see space through their hands.

In Korea's public-school classrooms and gifted-education programs, about a thousand students picked up those pieces. Small hands traced the wood grain, tried again, and heard the quiet click of a fit.

One student set the direction

When the founder was young, he saw a middle-school boy crying. The boy wanted to repair cars. But the adults around him said happiness meant becoming a civil servant or a doctor. That was Korea, back then. So the boy was folding away his dream.

That moment stayed with him. Education, he came to believe, should help one person properly.

Ten years went into the work

For ten years, In-Us sat with elementary, middle, and high school teachers and built learning tools for real classrooms. More than fifty programs reached students; more than a hundred, if the failed ones are counted too.

Together, they worked with 400+ schools, and hundreds of thousands of students learned with In-Us materials.

Now it rests beside you

Now the wooden puzzle lives as a calm 3D soma-cube game. Two hundred stages. Twelve languages. Offline. Dark mode. No timers, no lives, no failure screen, no forced ads.

It is made for the quiet before sleep: a piece turns, finds its place, and settles with a soft snap.

And when a stage ends, the game leaves you one quiet line — “Your effort shines quietly.” Two hundred stages, two hundred different words of encouragement.

Education is slow, but certain. When one person is cared for properly, warmth has somewhere to begin.
